# Pig

A simple dice-rolling game where you race against the computer to reach 100 points. Roll as many times as you like to build up points, but roll a 1 and you lose all points for that turn!

## How to play

1. Click **Roll** to roll the die.
2. If you roll a 2 through 6, that amount is added to your current turn total.
3. If you roll a **1**, your turn ends immediately and you lose all points accumulated during that turn.
4. Click **Hold** to bank your turn total into your overall score and hand the turn over to the computer.
5. The first player to reach **100 points** wins.
